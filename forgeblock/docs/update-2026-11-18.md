# ForgeBlock update: 18 November 2026

PR #10 (`dynamic-checks-and-gate`), merged into `main` as commit `6d26742`. 26 files changed.

## Summary of changes

### 1. Landing page, sign-in, terms and settings
- Removed the "Privacy and Help" and "The study" footer columns, and the sentence about ForgeBlock screening signatures and a qualified examiner making the final call (landing and sign-in pages).
- Removed the "Access control and ethical use" card from Policy and retention.
- Rewrote "Before you start" as general terms and conditions, with no mention of the prototype or the study.

### 2. Bad iPad references
- **Single-signature checks:** a pause over 3 s lowers the quality score and one over 8 s rejects the signature. A signature over 15 s and stray taps or palm touches also lower the score. A pen that sends constant pressure now counts as "no pressure".
- **Reference checks:** a reference that took much longer or shorter than the client's others, has a different number of pen lifts, or looks different gets an amber "Check this signature" badge with the reasons. This also works on references already saved. There's also a notice when a client's references mix pens with and without pressure/tilt.

### 3. Entirely different signatures come out Inconsistent
- A new shape check compares how the signature looks with the client's references.
- If the shape is clearly different, or 5 or more of the 7 handwriting features are outside the usual range:
  - An Inconclusive result that leans not genuine becomes Inconsistent.
  - A Consistent result goes to the examiner as Inconclusive.
- The result page explains why.
- There are on/off switches under **Policy and retention → Extra screening checks**, one for this dynamic check and one for the existing static check.

### 4. Palm rejection on the signing pad
- Pen-only mode turns on by itself when a pen is first used, remembered per device.
- The pad follows one touch at a time, and iOS palm cancellations end the stroke cleanly.

### 5. Behind the scenes
- The tests no longer upload to the real Supabase storage, which is what made them freeze.
- The paper notes have new sections 16 and 17 with the thresholds for Chapter 3.

Screening still uses the same model, thresholds and calibration. All 133 backend tests pass, and the frontend builds with no lint errors.
